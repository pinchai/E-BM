# Module 3 --- E-Commerce Platforms

**Duration:** 2 Hours

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

-   Explain what an e-commerce platform is.
-   Differentiate between an online store, website, mobile app,
    marketplace, and social commerce.
-   Identify popular e-commerce platforms.
-   Understand the main components of an e-commerce website.
-   Compare different online shopping platforms.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part 1: Introduction to E-Commerce Platforms (15 Minutes)

## What is an E-Commerce Platform?

An **E-Commerce Platform** is software that allows businesses to sell
products or services online.

It helps businesses:

-   Display products
-   Accept orders
-   Process payments
-   Manage customers
-   Track inventory

Examples:

-   Smile Shop
-   Brown Coffee Online Store
-   Sokly Phone WooCommerce Store
-   TikTok Shop Cambodia

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part 2: Types of E-Commerce Platforms (25 Minutes)

## 1. Online Store

An online store is a website or app owned by a business.

Example:

-   Chip Mong Retail Online
-   PPT Online Store
-   Ten11 Fashion Store

### Advantages

-   Full control
-   Own branding
-   Customer data belongs to owner

### Disadvantages

-   Need marketing
-   Need website management

## 2. Website

A website provides company information and may also sell products.

Examples

-   ABA Bank Website
-   Brown Coffee Website
-   BELTEI Website

Typical Pages

-   Home
-   About
-   Products
-   Contact

## 3. Mobile App

Customers shop using a smartphone application.

Examples

-   Smile Shop App
-   Little Fashion App
-   Brown Coffee App

Advantages

-   Fast
-   Push notifications
-   Better user experience

## 4. Marketplace

A marketplace is a platform where many sellers sell products together.

Examples

-   Smile Shop
-   Khmer24
-   Smile Shop
-   Little Fashion

Advantages

-   Large customer base
-   Easy to start selling

Disadvantages

-   High competition
-   Platform fees

## 5. Social Commerce

Selling products directly through social media.

Examples

-   Facebook Shop Cambodia
-   TikTok Shop Cambodia
-   Instagram Shop Cambodia

Advantages

-   Easy to reach customers
-   Live selling
-   Video marketing

Disadvantages

-   Depends on platform rules
-   Limited customization

# Part 3: Popular E-Commerce Platforms (30 Minutes)

## Shopify

-   Cloud-based
-   Easy to use
-   Beautiful themes
-   Secure payment
-   App Store

**Pros** - Beginner friendly - Fast setup - No coding required

**Cons** - Monthly subscription - Extra app costs

## WooCommerce

-   WordPress plugin
-   Free
-   Open source
-   Flexible
-   Thousands of plugins

## Magento

-   Enterprise platform
-   Highly customizable
-   Multi-store support
-   Large product catalog

## Wix

-   Drag-and-drop editor
-   Templates
-   Online payment
-   Beginner friendly

## TikTok Shop Cambodia

-   Live shopping
-   Affiliate marketing
-   Creator promotion
-   Short videos

## Facebook Shop Cambodia

-   Product catalog
-   Messenger integration
-   Marketplace integration

# Part 4: Website Components (30 Minutes)

## Homepage

-   Banner
-   Search bar
-   Categories
-   Featured products
-   Footer

## Product Page

-   Product image
-   Product name
-   Price
-   Description
-   Stock
-   Reviews
-   Add to Cart

## Shopping Cart

-   Add products
-   Remove products
-   Update quantity
-   View total price

## Checkout

-   Shipping address
-   Delivery method
-   Payment method
-   Order summary

## Payment

-   Credit Card
-   Debit Card
-   ABA Pay
-   Bank Transfer (ABA/ACLEDA)
-   QR Payment
-   Cash on Delivery (COD)

## Customer Account

-   Register
-   Login
-   View orders
-   Save addresses
-   Wishlist
-   Change password

### Shopping Flow

``` text
Homepage
   ↓
Browse Products
   ↓
Product Page
   ↓
Add to Cart
   ↓
Shopping Cart
   ↓
Checkout
   ↓
Payment
   ↓
Order Confirmation
```

  Criteria             Store A   Store B

### Discussion Questions

1.  Which platform is easier to use?
2.  Which has the better product search?
3.  Which offers more payment methods?
