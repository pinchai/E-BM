# E-Business Management Course

**Duration:** 45 Hours

## Target Audience

- Business students
- IT students
- Entrepreneurs
- Small business owners
- Beginners interested in online business

---

# Course Objectives

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

- Understand E-Business concepts
- Differentiate E-Business and E-Commerce
- Analyze different E-Business models
- Create an online business strategy
- Understand digital marketing fundamentals
- Learn online payment systems
- Manage customer relationships online
- Understand cybersecurity basics
- Learn logistics and supply chain management
- Create a simple E-Business project

---
