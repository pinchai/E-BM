# Module 1 — Introduction to E-Business

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

- Define Business and E-Business.
- Explain the evolution of E-Business.
- Compare Traditional Business with E-Business.
- indentify between E-Commerce and E-Business.
- Explain the importance, benefits, and challenges of E-Business.
- Identify current E-Business trends.
- Recognize local businesses that use E-Business.

# 1. What is Business?

A **Business** is an organization or activity that produces and sells goods or services to customers in order to earn a profit.

### Examples

- Restaurant
- Coffee shop
- Clothing store
- School
- Bank
- Hospital

### Main Components

- Customers
- Products or Services
- Employees
- Money
- Marketing
- Suppliers

```text
Supplier
    │
    ▼
Business
    │
    ▼
Customer
    │
    ▼
Payment
```

---

# 2. What is E-Business?

**Electronic Business (E-Business)** is the use of digital technologies and the Internet to perform and manage all business activities.

### Examples

- Online shopping
- Online payment
- Internet banking
- Customer support
- Inventory management
- Online marketing
- Supply chain management

```text
Customer
      │
      ▼
Website / Mobile App
      │
      ▼
Business System
      │
      ▼
Warehouse / Payment / Delivery
```

# 4. Traditional Business vs E-Business

| Traditional Business | E-Business |
|----------------------|------------|
| Physical store | Website/App |
| Limited working hours | 24/7 availability |
| Local customers | Global customers |
| Cash payment | Digital payment |
| Paper records | Digital records |
| Higher operating costs | Lower operating costs |
| Face-to-face communication | Online communication |

---

# 5. E-Commerce vs E-Business

| E-Commerce | E-Business |
|------------|------------|
| Buying and selling online | All business activities using technology |
| Focuses on transactions | Covers the entire business process |
| Online payment | Marketing, HR, inventory, CRM, finance |
| Smaller scope | Broader scope |

> **E-Commerce is a part of E-Business.**
