# 1. What is a Business Model?

A **Business Model** describes how a company creates value, delivers
products or services to customers, and earns money.

It answers questions such as:

-   Who are the customers?
-   What products or services are offered?
-   How are products delivered?
-   How does the company make profit?

## Simple Formula

``` text
Customer
    ↓
Value (Product/Service)
    ↓
Payment
    ↓
Revenue
```

### Example

A coffee shop: - Customers buy coffee. - Coffee shop provides
beverages. - Customers pay cash. - Business earns profit.

Online example (GrabFood): - Restaurants provide food. - Grab delivers
it. - Customers order through the app. - Grab earns delivery fees and
commissions.

# 2. Types of E-Business Models

## B2B (Business-to-Business)

Business sells products or services to another business.

``` text
Business → Business
```

**Examples** - Microsoft - Cisco - Alibaba Wholesale - SAP

**Characteristics** - Large orders - Long-term relationships -
Contract-based - Higher transaction values

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## B2C (Business-to-Consumer)

Business sells directly to consumers.

``` text
Business → Customer
```

**Examples** - Amazon - Apple Store - Netflix - Nike

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## C2C (Consumer-to-Consumer)

Consumers sell to other consumers.

``` text
Customer → Customer
```

**Examples** - eBay - Facebook Marketplace - Craigslist - Khmer24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## C2B (Consumer-to-Business)

Individuals provide products or services to businesses.

``` text
Customer → Business
```

**Examples** - Fiverr - Upwork - Shutterstock Contributors

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## B2G (Business-to-Government)

Businesses provide products or services to government organizations.

``` text
Business → Government
```

**Examples** - Government software vendors - Medical equipment
suppliers - Construction companies

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## G2C (Government-to-Citizen)

Government provides online services to citizens.

``` text
Government → Citizen
```

**Examples** - Online tax payment - Passport application - Driver's
license renewal

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Business Model Comparison

  Model   Seller       Buyer        Example
  ------- ------------ ------------ ----------------------
  B2B     Business     Business     Alibaba Wholesale
  B2C     Business     Consumer     Amazon
  C2C     Consumer     Consumer     Facebook Marketplace
  C2B     Consumer     Business     Fiverr
  B2G     Business     Government   Government IT Vendor
  G2C     Government   Citizen      Online Tax Service

# 3. Revenue Models

## Sales

Earn money by selling products.

Examples: - Amazon - Apple - Nike

## Subscription

Customers pay monthly or yearly.

Examples: - Netflix - Spotify - Microsoft 365 - Adobe Creative Cloud

## Advertisement

Earn money from displaying advertisements.

Examples: - Google - Facebook - YouTube

## Affiliate Marketing

Earn commissions by recommending products.

Examples: - Amazon Associates - Product review websites

## Marketplace

Connect buyers and sellers and charge transaction fees.

Examples: - Amazon Marketplace - Grab - Foodpanda - Airbnb

## Freemium

Offer free basic features and paid premium features.

Examples: - Canva - Dropbox - Grammarly - Zoom

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Revenue Model Comparison

  Revenue Model   How Money is Earned   Example
  --------------- --------------------- -------------------
  Sales           Product sales         Amazon
  Subscription    Monthly/Yearly fee    Netflix
  Advertisement   Ads                   Google
  Affiliate       Commission            Amazon Associates
  Marketplace     Transaction fee       Grab
  Freemium        Premium upgrade       Canva

# 4. Case Studies

## Amazon

-   Business Models: B2C, B2B, Marketplace
-   Revenue: Product sales, Prime subscription, AWS, advertising

## Alibaba

-   Business Models: B2B, B2C, Marketplace
-   Revenue: Marketplace commissions, advertising, cloud services

## Shopify

-   Business Model: B2B
-   Revenue: Subscription, payment processing, app marketplace

## Netflix

-   Business Model: B2C
-   Revenue: Subscription, advertising

## Grab

-   Business Models: B2C, Marketplace
-   Revenue: Ride commissions, delivery fees, financial services

## Foodpanda

-   Business Models: B2C, Marketplace
-   Revenue: Delivery fees, restaurant commissions, advertising
